Operator proposal — Step 47 revision, published 2026-08-05

Stop patching Step 47. Decide the policy, split the gate, merge the migrator.

Five review rounds could not converge one step doing two unrelated jobs. This revision decides the transaction drift policy once, sends the migrator back through one bounded round, and moves the static containment gate to its own step off the cutover critical path. Full rationale: documentation/step-47-decomposition-decision.md.

What I heard

Do not run a sixth round

Rounds 1–5 each patched what the previous round found, and the recurring defect shape was introduced by fix rounds three times. The deliverable is a decision, not another diff.

The branch is not mergeable as-is

build-step-1785890195 holds real wins, but round 5 proved its final commit regressed the exact path it was built to repair.

The sibling-asymmetry shape is the enemy

A consumer-home path escaping a check its sibling branch already performs — operator-flagged, and the thing every round kept reintroducing.

Why this shape

Round 4 (reviewing 5ef1045) returned a single Block demanding that preserve-drift escalate; round 5 (reviewing the fix) condemned exactly that escalation. Two consecutive independent rounds Block'd opposite policies on the same hunk — proof the drift policy was never decided, and that the code's two conflicting undo wrappers were encoding a disagreement no patch could resolve. Separately, the static containment gate (born mid-build as a stop-and-audit remedy) sat in no step's Done-when, so gate-completeness findings had no acceptance criteria to be bounded against and kept blocking the migrator's merge.

D13Split tool from gate

What: Step 47 keeps the migrator + engine and merges alone; the containment gate's improvement becomes Step 47b with its own Done-when, off the 48–50 critical path.

D14Three-case drift policy

What: escalate to failed_incomplete only for (1) mutated-path content-identity violations and (2) post-install verification failures on preserved paths after a fully-applied transaction (bounded to the failure set, with a corrected message). (3) Preserve drift during a pre-completion abort lands rolled_back + an advisory; a follow-up bare -Apply re-plans and converges.

Why: rolled_back honestly claims only "every byte this tool mutated is restored" — the no-payload preserve design makes any broader claim unprovable. This adjudicates the round-4/round-5 oscillation.

Tweak: escalate on all preserve drift (round 4's position), or demote case 2 to advisory as well.

D15Resume from 5ef1045

What: restore both round-5-changed files to their 5ef1045 state and build the policy from there; the branch's earlier wins (choke-point fix, shared content-identity rule, skill-mesh-discovery.ps1, ~100 tests, #89) all predate that commit and are kept.

Why: round 4 found that tree one Block from clean; the bde8a5f delta is the round that regressed.

D16Gate demoted; AST deferred

What: the gate becomes an honest best-effort tripwire (completeness claim removed, blind-spot classes documented); the sound AST-based rewrite is deferred behind a named trigger — a live false negative in either half of the invariant.

Why: every known hole is latent (zero live instances), runtime protections are the consumer-machine controls, and an AST mini-tool's own review burden is the exact dynamic that consumed rounds 3–5.

Tweak: schedule the AST rewrite now, or delete the gate entirely.

D1747b acceptance = differential corpus + tripwire

What: 47b's no-weakening criterion is keyed to a committed corpus containing the round-5 counterexamples, which the rejected bde8a5f gate must demonstrably fail; plus a content-identity tripwire enumerating Invoke-ActionUndo's destroying/overwriting branches.
